Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new project? +
Click on “New Project” from the dashboard or editor, choose the language, and enter the details.
How do I share a project? +
Go to the Projects page → click the share icon → share ID + password or invite via email.
What is the commit/review system? +
Owners commit directly. Collaborators propose changes. Owners approve/reject in Code Review page.
How does the AI assistant work? +
Click the AI icon in the editor panel, describe your issue, and receive suggestions or fixes.
Is my code secure? +
Yes — projects are private by default and all activities are logged for security.
